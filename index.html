ABOUT PROJECT

🌿 About Herbal Plants

Learning about nature and useful plants around us.

🌱

What Are Herbal Plants?

Herbal plants are plants that are commonly used for traditional health, food, beauty and other useful purposes.

💚

Why Are They Important?

They provide useful natural resources and help people understand the importance of plants and biodiversity.

🌍

Community Awareness

Our project helps students and communities learn about herbal plants and responsible plant cultivation.

HEALTH & USES

💚 Herbal Plant Benefits

Commonly known uses and benefits.

❤️

Health Support

Many plants are traditionally used as part of healthy lifestyles.

🧠

Mind & Wellness

Some herbs are traditionally associated with relaxation and wellness.

🛡️

Traditional Immunity Uses

Several plants are traditionally used in home practices.

🍵

Herbal Drinks

Plants such as mint, ginger and lemongrass can be used in drinks.

💆

Beauty Uses

Aloe vera and hibiscus have traditional beauty-related uses.

🥗

Food Uses

Many herbs and spices are useful ingredients in everyday food.

Leaves

🌱

CHALLENGES

⚠️ Problems & Challenges

🌱

Lack of Awareness

People may not know about useful plants around them.

🏙️

Urbanization

Increasing construction can reduce green spaces.

💧

Water Problems

Insufficient water can affect plant growth.

🗑️

Pollution

Pollution can negatively affect plants and soil.

🌳

Loss of Traditional Knowledge

Traditional knowledge about plants may not always be passed to younger generations.

🐛

Pests

Insects and diseases can affect healthy plant growth.

About Herbal Plants

Understanding their importance

🌿

What are Herbal Plants?

Herbal plants are plants that can be used for traditional and medicinal purposes. They have been used by people for generations.

Learning about these plants helps us understand their value and encourages their proper conservation.

💊

Medicinal Value

Many herbal plants are traditionally used for different health and wellness purposes.

🌍

Environmental Value

Plants support biodiversity and contribute to a healthy environment.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Community Value

Herbal plants can support community awareness, home gardening and traditional knowledge.

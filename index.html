ABOUT PROJECT

What Are Herbal Plants?

🌿

Natural Plants

Herbal plants are natural plants that contain useful compounds and are traditionally used for health purposes.

💚

Natural Healing

Many herbal plants are traditionally used in home remedies and natural health practices.

🌍

Environment

Growing herbal plants can support biodiversity and encourage more natural and organic farming practices.

🌱 Introduction

Herbal plants have been used for thousands of years. They are important in traditional systems such as Ayurveda, Siddha and Unani. Herbal plants are also used in food, oils, cosmetics and health products.
